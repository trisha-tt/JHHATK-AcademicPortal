Topic:
Our topic is similar to Topic 5: Patient-Doctor Portal, though slightly changed to be used in an academic setting. 
Our project, "Academic Portal," will help students connect with WSU professors and tutors online for questions or quick explanations.
Students can filter by department and class. Each class has its forum and students can post their questions to it for professors/tutors to reply.
They also have the option to contact a specific tutor by personal message.

We aim to create a web app that attempts to link students with tutors. Willing professors and tutors would register their contact details and would receive messages from students with problems.
Professors and TAs of a certain department (category) have access to only the forums of the category and class(es) they teach, restricting access from outsiders. 
Students can either post their questions publicly or privately (visible to only the professors/tutors). 
If they choose to post it publicly, other students can see their questions and their response to it. Other students will not be able to comment/reply to these messages.  
It is a very practical project that would help a large number of students.
