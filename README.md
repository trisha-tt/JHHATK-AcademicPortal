Topic:
Our project is a web application that can allow students to check in to the MLC and post their questions to the portal, allowing the tutors present in the center to know how many students are waiting for their help. The admin of the system and the tutors of the MLC will be required to log in to enter this information through the login page.
Once the student checks in, they can raise a “flag” on the web app (from the home page) which signals to the tutors that they need help. Tutors can view how many “flags” are raised, and what the student’s question is. Then, the tutor can accept the request on the software and go help the student at their table. 
After the question has been answered, the software will allow the tutor to enter some details about what they did. This will be helpful for the MLC to track how many students are coming in and for which class they are coming in. We plan to implement it in such a way that it allows the MLC staff and other higher-ups to view statistics in real-time, in the form of reports.
